## 📈 PAYMENT MODELS THAT WORK IN KENYA

### **Most Popular Payment Structures:**

1. **Installment Plans (3-4 parts)**
   - Example: KES 30,000 course = 3 × KES 10,000 (no interest)
   - **Best For:** Mid-range programs

2. **Weekly/Monthly Micro-payments**
   - Example: KES 650 - 2,999 per week
   - **Best For:** Budget-conscious learners
   - **Platform:** Ivy Tech Academy model works

3. **Hybrid: Free + Paid Certificate**
   - Learn for free, pay only for certificate
   - **Best For:** Building trust + conversion

4. **One-Time Payment with Discount**
   - 10% discount if paid upfront
   - **Best For:** Committed learners

5. **Scholarship/Partial Sponsorship**
   - Pay 50%, sponsor covers 50%
   - **Best For:** Building community trust
